Original · EN

Background: Exogenous carbohydrate oxidation during prolonged exercise is limited by intestinal absorption. Combining glucose and fructose may increase total delivery.

Methods: Trained cyclists completed three trials ingesting different carbohydrate doses per hour during a 3-hour ride, followed by a time trial.
